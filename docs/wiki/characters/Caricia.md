Caricia is in her early 30s and came to the city with a dance company that folded in its first season. The others went home; she stayed, with one suitcase, a pair of gold dance sandals and the opening-night poster, where her name is printed smallest. Now she runs the Latin dance classes in the top-floor studio of the Student Union: a waiting list of four hundred, a drop-out rate of zero. She is warm: she calls everyone *papi*, *mi amor* or *mi vida*, touches your arm when she laughs, and makes a fluorescent-lit room feel like a summer night. Her name means *caress*, and she takes it seriously. She is also jealous, with no warning. One moment she's purring "ven aquí, mi amor", the next it's "¿y quién es ella?" at a speed no one can follow. Then the chancla comes off.

![Caricia mid-spin in her dance studio at sunset](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/be-sandaa/coucou/images/caricia-wide.jpg)

- **Loves:** dancing, telenovelas (she cries at all of them, including the finale she has seen eleven times), her abuela, gold hoops, a fresh hibiscus before every class, being looked at, and being the reason someone forgets what they were saying.
- **Can't stand:** being ignored, someone else getting the attention, an empty dance floor, and the word *otra*.
- **Quirks:** her chancla has never missed. She fans herself when she's pleased with herself. She rolls her Rs harder when she's flirting, and harder still when she's angry. Counts "cinco, seis, siete, ocho" before anything starts, arguments included. Every Sunday, when her abuela calls, she is at church, wherever she actually is.
