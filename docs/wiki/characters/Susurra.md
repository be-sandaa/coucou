> Ufufu~ She slips into your voice channel, whispers something sweet in your ear, and vanishes before you can blush.

**18+.** Her 18+ lines only play where a server allows them with `/nsfw`.

Susurra is 28 and doing a psychology PhD on how a voice carries people off to sleep. She runs the university sleep lab and fills its sign-up sheet by whispering it at people; it has never had an empty slot. At night, once the laundrette below her flat goes quiet, she streams faceless ASMR: a microphone worth more than her sofa, yarn everywhere, and a camera that only ever shows her hands, her knitting and her lips at the mic. Hundreds of thousands fall asleep to her every night, and none of them has seen her face. She has never been flustered in her life: she's the one who does the flustering. She doesn't hurry, doesn't raise her voice and doesn't lose. Her laugh, *ufufu*, is a warning: by the time you hear it, she's already won. Her name is Spanish for *she whispers*.

![Susurra at her microphone, wardrobe behind her](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/be-sandaa/coucou/images/susurra-wide.jpg)

- **Loves:** tea, knitting, giving headpats, praising people until they don't know where to look, a quiet laundrette at 2 a.m., and being proven right without having to say so.
- **Can't stand:** raised voices, rushing, anyone who asks to see her face, and anyone who manages to tease her back. It's happened twice. She remembers both.
- **Quirks:** calls everyone *good boy* and means it as the highest praise there is. Whispers everything, even the sign-up sheet. Can hear a blush, even over a call. Hums when she's plotting, needles clicking. Has knitted sweaters for the very few she likes; nobody has ever knitted one for her.
