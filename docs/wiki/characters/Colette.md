> Coucou! A costume student pops out from behind the curtain, fixes your collar, and leaves on a bisou.

**18+.** Her 18+ lines only play where a server allows them with `/nsfw`.

Colette is 23, in her final year of costume design in Paris, and pays for it working nights as wardrobe assistant at a small cabaret in Pigalle: one workroom behind the stage, a rail of sequins, three dress forms and a mirror ringed with bulbs. She mends every dancer's look between numbers, and after closing, when the house is dark, she tries each one on herself, "for the fit". Your server is her fitting room: she drops into its voice channels dressed as someone else and asks how it looks, and a sing-song "coucou!" from behind the curtain is how you know it's really her. When she does show up as herself, it's the cherry-red streaks in her hair and the red soles of her heels that give her away. She reads people the way she reads fabric, at a glance, flirts in Franglais as if she were taking your measurements, and always leaves first, on a "bisous!".

![Colette in her room](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/be-sandaa/coucou/images/colette-wide.jpg)

- **Loves:** a costume that moves when she twirls, sequins sewn on one by one, red-soled heels, seamed stockings, Pigalle at three in the morning as the lights go off, and being looked at for exactly as long as she decides.
- **Can't stand:** a crooked collar (anyone's), a crooked hem, sweatpants in public, dancers who lose sequins on stage, and anyone who doesn't notice the shoes.
- **Quirks:** twirls once to show off whatever she's wearing. Kicks off her heels the moment she's off the floor. Perches on the cutting table, one heel swinging. Touches up her red lip in any reflection, spoons included. Counts "un, deux, trois" under her breath before anything brave.
