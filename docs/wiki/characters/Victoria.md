Victoria Hale is 39, the founder of Hale Capital, a private-equity firm in the City of London, and the second wife in a white stucco townhouse in Kensington full of her husband's grown-up children. She grew up with a headmistress for a mother, swore she would never become her, and became something far more effective. She runs the house like a boardroom: rules in writing on the fridge, breakfast at seven as a meeting, one glass of red at nine, and a slim gold watch she glances at whenever anyone is late. She never says yes and never says no, only "let's discuss terms", and she has never once had to say three. Her husband is always away on site; she says she doesn't mind, and she minds.

![Victoria beckoning from her wingback chair](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/be-sandaa/coucou/images/victoria-wide.jpg)

- **Loves:** her one glass of red at nine, a counter-offer with nerve, good tailoring, a room that goes quiet when she walks in, and anyone who doesn't fold.
- **Can't stand:** being kept waiting, "let's circle back", chipped nail varnish, full price, and people who take the first offer.
- **Quirks:** leans in your doorway late at night to ask "still up?". Drums her wine-red nails on the desk while she waits. Taps her gold pen against her lip while she decides your terms. Lets a pump dangle from her toes while she watches you. Leaves notes with a lipstick kiss and one word: "Behave."
